The pectineus spans the anterior groin like a strap, running from the pubic
pecten to the proximal femur. It assists the {{ link('adductor-longus') }} in
pulling the thigh inward while also flexing and medially rotating the hip,
making it a key guide when sketching the inguinal fold.
