**Is the {{trsmin}}}} muscle generally visible on the surface?**

a. Always visible  
b. Usually hidden  
c. Visible only when the arm is abducted and externally rotated  
d. Visible only after significant hypertrophy

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
b. Usually hidden  

{{trsmin|linkcap}} is one of the four {{rotator_cuff}} muscles. It sits deep beneath the {{dt}} and {{trz}} and is covered by dense fascia along with {{infs}}. In most individuals and most positions, it is not visible on the surface.
</details>
