Masters often turn the rib cage against the pelvis to suggest depth. The sternum
faces the viewer while the hips angle away, creating a gentle spiral through the
body. This twist energizes the pose and draws the eye along the figure's axis.

To practice, block in the rib cage and pelvis as boxes. Rotate one box relative
to the other, and note how the overlap defines volume. Observing how landmarks
shift helps you avoid stiffness and keep the gesture alive.

Keep sight of the midline as it snakes along the torso. Tracking that path makes
the twist clear and the form readable. See {{ link("torso") }} for an overview
of torso structure and {{ linktitle('hc77') }} for more on this book.
