## Summary

<table>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <th>Origin</th>
      <td>
        <ol>
          <li>External surfaces of the upper 8–9 ribs</li>
        </ol>
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th>Insertion</th>
      <td>
        <ol>
          <li>Anterior (costal) surface of medial border of scapula</li>
        </ol>
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th>Actions</th>
      <td>
        <ol>
          <li>Protracts (abducts) the scapula</li>
          <li>Upward rotation of the scapula</li>
          <li>Holds scapula against thoracic wall</li>
          <li>Accessory muscle of inspiration (elevates ribs)</li>
        </ol>
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
