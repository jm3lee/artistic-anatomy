## Summary

### Origins

Medial {{epicondyle|link}} of the {{humerus|linkinternal}} via the common flexor
tendon

### Insertions

Base of the second and third metacarpals

### Actions

<ol>
<li>Wrist flexion</li>
<li>Wrist abduction</li>
</ol>
