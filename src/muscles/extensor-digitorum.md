The extensor digitorum lies in the posterior forearm and sends four thin
tendons to the back of the hand. Each tendon crosses the dorsal wrist and
inserts into the extensor expansions of digits two through five, allowing them
to straighten at the metacarpophalangeal joints.

It shares a common origin at the lateral epicondyle with [extensor carpi
ulnaris](extensor-carpi-ulnaris.html) and
[extensor digiti minimi](extensor-digiti-minimi.html). The muscle is an
antagonist to the [flexor digitorum
superficialis](flexor-digitorum-superficialis.html).

* **Origin:** lateral epicondyle of the humerus.
* **Insertion:** extensor expansions of digits two through five.
* **Action:** finger [extension](../keyterms/#extension) and weak wrist
  extension.
