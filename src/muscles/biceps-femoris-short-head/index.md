The short head of the biceps femoris reinforces the lateral hamstring
complex without crossing the hip joint. Its fibers spring from the distal
{{ linkicon('femur') }} and dive into the common tendon on the head of the
{{ linkicon('fibula') }}, helping the long head flex and laterally rotate the
knee.
