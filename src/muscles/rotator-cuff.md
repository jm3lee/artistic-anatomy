The rotator cuff comprises four scapular muscles that stabilize the
{{ linktitle('glenohumeral-joint') }} and move the {{ linkicon('humerus') }}.

## Muscles

- **{{ linktitle('sups') }}**
  - Origin: Supraspinous fossa of the {{ linktitle('scapula') }}
  - Insertion: Superior facet of the greater tubercle of the
    {{ linkicon('humerus') }}
  - Action: Abducts the {{ linkicon('humerus') }}; stabilizes the
    {{ linktitle('glenohumeral-joint') }}

- **{{ linktitle('infs') }}**
  - Origin: Infraspinous fossa of the {{ linktitle('scapula') }}
  - Insertion: Middle facet of the greater tubercle of the
    {{ linkicon('humerus') }}
  - Action: Laterally rotates the {{ linkicon('humerus') }}; stabilizes the
    {{ linktitle('glenohumeral-joint') }}

- **{{ linktitle('trsmin') }}**
  - Origin: Lateral border of the {{ linktitle('scapula') }}
  - Insertion: Inferior facet of the greater tubercle of the
    {{ linkicon('humerus') }}
  - Action: Laterally rotates and assists adduction of the
    {{ linkicon('humerus') }}; stabilizes the
    {{ linktitle('glenohumeral-joint') }}

- **{{ linktitle('subscp') }}**
  - Origin: Subscapular fossa (anterior {{ linktitle('scapula') }})
  - Insertion: Lesser tubercle of the {{ linkicon('humerus') }}
  - Action: Medially rotates and adducts the {{ linkicon('humerus') }};
    stabilizes the {{ linktitle('glenohumeral-joint') }}
