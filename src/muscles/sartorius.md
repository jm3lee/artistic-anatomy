## Summary

<table>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <th>Origin</th>
      <td>Anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS) of the pelvic bone</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th>Insertion</th>
      <td>Medial surface of proximal tibia (pes anserinus area)</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th>Actions</th>
      <td>
        <ol>
          <li>Flexion of the hip</li>
          <li>Abduction of the hip</li>
          <li>Lateral (external) rotation of the hip</li>
          <li>Flexion of the knee</li>
          <li>Medial rotation of the tibia on the femur (when knee is flexed)</li>
        </ol>
      </td>
    </tr>
