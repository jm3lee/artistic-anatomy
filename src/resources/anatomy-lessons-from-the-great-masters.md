## Bibliography Entry

Hale, Robert Beverly, and Terence Coyle. ***Anatomy Lessons from the Great Masters: 100 Great Figure Drawings Analyzed***. New York: Watson-Guptill Publications, 2000.

## Review

Highly recommended for deepening your understanding of human anatomy, this book
offers exceptional insights through detailed illustrations and commentary. The
authors pair original works with clear anatomical line drawings, providing
explanations that clarify the structure shown in each drawing. I often revisit
this dense resource, and each reading reveals new insights into artistic
anatomy.
