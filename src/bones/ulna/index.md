## Bony Landmarks

### Coronoid Process {{'{#coronoid_process}'}}
The {{anterior|link}} projection just distal to the trochlear notch; it helps form the elbow joint and provides attachment for the {{brc|linktitle}}.

### Head of the Ulna  
The distal, rounded end that articulates with the ulnar notch of the radius and with the triangular fibrocartilage complex at the wrist.

### Interosseous Border  
The sharp {{medial|link}} ridge running along the shaft where the interosseous membrane attaches, linking ulna to radius.

### Olecranon Process {{'{#olecranon_process}'}}
The large, {{posterior|link}} “hook” at the elbow that forms the point of the elbow and serves as the triceps’ {{insertion|link}} site.

Origins:

- {{fcu|linktitle}}
- {{trc|linktitle}}

Insertions:

- {{anc|linktitle}}

### Radial Notch  
A shallow depression on the {{lateral|link}} side of the coronoid process where the head of the radius rests, forming the proximal radioulnar joint.

### Styloid Process  
A pointed projection on the {{medial|link}} side of the ulnar head that serves as an attachment for the ulnar collateral {{ligament|link}} of the wrist.

### Supinator Crest  
A ridge on the {{lateral|link}} proximal ulna extending down from the radial notch; it provides {{origin|link}} for the supinator muscle.

### Trochlear (Semilunar) Notch  
The C-shaped articular surface just below the olecranon that grips the humeral trochlea.

### Ulnar Tuberosity {{'{#ulnar_tuberosity}'}}
A roughened area just below the coronoid process on the {{anterior|link}} shaft.
{{brc|linktitle}} and flexor digitorum profundus attach here.

## Muscle Origins

### By Bony Landmarks

* **Coronoid Process ({{medial|linktitle}} border)**
  * {{protrs|linktitle}} (ulnar head)

* **{{posterior|linktitle}} border of ulnar shaft**
  * {{fcu|linktitle}} (ulnar head)

* **Shaft (interosseous border)**
  * Extensor indicis (via interosseous membrane attachment)

* **Shaft ({{posterior|linktitle}} surface)**
  * Extensor pollicis longus (via interosseous membrane attachment)

* **Supinator crest (proximal ulna)**
  * Supinator

## Muscle Insertions

### By Bony Landmarks

* **Coronoid process and ulnar tuberosity**  
  * {{brc|linktitle}}  

* **Lateral aspect of [olecranon process](#olecranon_process)**  
  * {{anc|linktitle}}

* **[Olecranon Process](#olecranon_process)**  
  * {{trc|linktitle}} (all heads)  

* **{{posterior|linktitle}} border of ulna**
  * Flexor carpi ulnaris (via pisiform, hamate, 5th metacarpal)  

* **Ulnar shaft (anteromedial surface)**  
  * Flexor digitorum superficialis (via intermediate phalanges; indirect insertion)  

* **Ulnar tuberosity (anteromedial shaft)**  
  * Flexor digitorum profundus (ulnar half)
