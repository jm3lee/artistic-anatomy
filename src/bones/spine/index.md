| Column Section   | Abbreviation | Vertebrae (typical) | Notes                                   |
|------------------|--------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Cervical         | C1–C7        | 7                   | Includes atlas (C1) and axis (C2)       |
| Thoracic         | T1–T12       | 12                  | Articulate with ribs                    |
| Lumbar           | L1–L5        | 5                   | Largest movable vertebrae               |
| Sacral           | S1–S5        | 5 (fused)           | Fused into single sacrum bone           |
| Coccygeal        | Co1–Co4¹     | 3–5 (typically 4)   | Fused into coccyx (“tailbone”)          |

¹ The coccygeal count varies between individuals (3–5), most commonly four.  

Altogether, adults have **33 vertebrae** before fusion; functionally there are 24 movable vertebrae (C, T, L) plus the sacrum and coccyx.
