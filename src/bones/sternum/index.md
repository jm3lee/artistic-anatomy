## Summary

The sternum forms the front of the rib cage and anchors the ribs and clavicles
at the body's midline. It consists of three fused segments:

- **Manubrium** – the upper portion bearing the jugular notch and articulating with the clavicles.
- **Body (Gladiolus)** – the elongated middle portion where ribs 2–7 attach via costal cartilage.
- **Xiphoid Process** – the small inferior tip.

Together these parts create a flat surface that marks the center of the thorax
and serves as a reference plane for the chest muscles.
