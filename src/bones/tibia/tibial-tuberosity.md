Also known as the **tuberosity of the tibia** or the **tibial tubercle**.

The tibial tuberosity is a bony bump on the front of the shinbone, just below
the knee. It connects to the patellar tendon, which helps the knee straighten.
This bump affects the shape of the leg and knee, changing how light and shadows
fall on the lower leg.
