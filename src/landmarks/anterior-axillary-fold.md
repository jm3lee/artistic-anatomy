## Summary

The **anterior axillary fold** is the fleshy border of the armpit formed by the
lower fibers of the {{ link('pecmaj') }} as they converge on the upper humerus.
The underlying {{ link('pecmin') }} and fascia thicken this edge, creating a
curved buttress that frames the front wall of the axilla against the rib cage.

Have the model press the elbow forward or raise the arm and the fold leaps into
relief, showing how the chest drapes over the shoulder joint. It blends with the
{{ link('dt') }} above yet leaves a soft groove, the deltopectoral triangle,
that guides you to the {{ link('clavicle') }} and to the hollow leading into the
axilla. When the arm relaxes, watch how the fold collapses into gentle planes
that still echo the sweeping pull of the pectoral mass.
