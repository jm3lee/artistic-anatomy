author: Brian Lee

:man:

| Plane                     | Divides Body Into     | Movements (Examples)                          |
|---------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| **Sagittal**              | Left / Right          | Flexion & Extension (nodding, elbow bend)     |
| **Frontal (Coronal)**     | Front / Back          | Abduction & Adduction (lifting arm to side)   |
| **Transverse (Horizontal)** | Top / Bottom        | Rotation (twisting at the waist)              |
| **Oblique**               | Diagonal slices       | Combined movements (reaching across the body) |
