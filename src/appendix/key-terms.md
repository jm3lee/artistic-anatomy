**Glenohumeral**  
From Greek **glēnē** “socket” (referring to the glenoid cavity of the scapula) +
Latin **humerus** “upper‐arm bone,” denoting the joint between the scapula’s
socket and the humerus.

**Interosseous membrane**  
A strong, fibrous syndesmosis spanning the shafts of the radius and ulna that
stabilizes the two bones, transmits load between them, and allows controlled
twisting during pronation and supination.
