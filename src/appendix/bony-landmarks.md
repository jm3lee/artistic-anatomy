## Head and Neck

* Cranium:
  * Frontal bone: Supraorbital ridge (brow ridge)
  * Parietal bones: Lateral and superior cranial curvature
  * Occipital bone: External occipital protuberance (inion)
* Zygomatic bone:
  * Zygomatic arch (cheekbone)
* Maxilla:
  * Infraorbital margin (under the eye socket)
* Mandible:
  * Mental protuberance (chin)
  * Angle of the mandible (jawline curve)
  * Mandibular ramus and condyle (side of jaw near the ear)
* Temporal bone:
  * Mastoid process (behind the ear)

## Thorax (Chest)

* Sternum:
  * Jugular notch (top of the sternum)
  * Sternal body
  * Xiphoid process (bottom tip of the sternum)
* Ribs:
  * Costal arch/margin (lower edge of the rib cage)

## Spine

* Vertebral column:
  * Cervical vertebrae (notably C7, the most prominent at the base of the neck)
  * Thoracic vertebrae: Spinous processes (visible as back bumps)
  * Lumbar vertebrae: Spinous processes (lower back bumps)
  * Sacrum: Sacral crest
  * Coccyx (tailbone)

## Shoulder and Upper Limb

* Clavicle:
  * Sternal end (connects to sternum)
  * Acromial end (connects to scapula)
* Scapula:
  * Acromion (top of shoulder blade)
  * Coracoid process (small hook-like projection)
  * Medial border (inner edge of the scapula)
  * Inferior angle (lower tip of the scapula)
* Humerus:
  * Greater tubercle (shoulder bump near the deltoid)
  * Medial epicondyle (inner elbow)
  * Lateral epicondyle (outer elbow)
* Ulna:
  * Olecranon (elbow point)
  * Styloid process (distal end, near the wrist)
* Radius:
  * Radial head (near the elbow)
  * Styloid process (distal end, near the wrist)

## Pelvis

* Ilium:
  * Iliac crest (hip crest)
  * Anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS)
* Ischium:
  * Ischial tuberosities ("sitting bones")
* Pubis:
  * Pubic symphysis (front joint of the pelvis)

## Lower Limb

* Femur:
  * Greater trochanter (prominent lateral bump near the hip)
  * Lateral and medial condyles (lower end of femur near the knee)
* Patella (Kneecap):
  * Base and apex (top and bottom points)
* Tibia:
  * Tibial tuberosity (front bump below the kneecap)
  * Medial malleolus (inner ankle)
* Fibula:
  * {{lateral_malleolus|linktitle}} (outer ankle)

## Hands

* Carpals:
  * Palpable bony prominences (scaphoid and pisiform near the wrist)
* Metacarpals and Phalanges:
  * Knuckles (heads of metacarpals)

## Feet

* Tarsals:
  * Calcaneus (heel bone)
  * Talus (top of the foot near the ankle)
* Metatarsals:
  * Heads (ball of the foot)
* Phalanges:
  * Toe joints
