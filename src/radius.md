## Muscles With Insertion on Radius

| Muscle | Insertion | Function |
|---|---|---|
| {{bc|linktitle}} | Radial tuberosity, bicipital aponeurosis | Supination of the forearm and elbow flexion |
| {{protrs}} | Lateral surface of the radius (midshaft) | Pronates and weakly flexes the forearm |
| [Supinator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supinator_muscle) &ast; | Proximal lateral radius | Supination of the forearm |
| {{brr|linktitle}} | Styloid process of radius | Flexion of the forearm at the elbow |

&ast; Supinator is a _deep_ muscle and shown here for completeness.
